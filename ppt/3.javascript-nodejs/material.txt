1992 — Mosaic Browser
  ↓
National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA),
University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign developed Mosaic,
one of the first web browsers.

1994 — Netscape Communicator founded
  ↓
The Mosaic team created Netscape and launched
Netscape Navigator — a popular browser at the time.

1995 — Brendan Eich hired
  ↓
Eich created a new language:
  Java + Scheme + Self = Mocha (in just 10 days!)
  ↓
Mocha → renamed to LiveScript
  ↓
LiveScript → renamed to JavaScript
  ↓
Netscape partnered with Sun Microsystems (creator of Java)
to compete with Microsoft.

1996 — Microsoft released JScript
  ↓
Microsoft introduced its own version for Internet Explorer.

1996 — Netscape sought standardization
  ↓
W3C rejected the proposal, so Netscape turned to ECMA International.
  ↓
The official standard was approved as ECMAScript
(because Java was still licensed by Sun Microsystems).

1997 — ECMAScript 1 (ES1)
1998 — ECMAScript 2 (ES2)
1999 — ECMAScript 3 (ES3)
2005 — AJAX (new technology boosting JavaScript)
2008 — ECMAScript 4 (cancelled)
2009 — ECMAScript 5 (ES5)
2015 — ECMAScript 6 (ES6 / ES2015)
2016 — ECMAScript 7 (ES7 / ES2016)
